# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other side is trying to help, is short on time, and may
not share your first language.

## What that means here

Criticise the code, not the person who wrote it. "This deletes reviews when the source
returns a short page" is useful; "this is amateur work" is not, and says nothing a
maintainer can act on.

Explain your reasoning. A review comment that only says what to change leaves the author
guessing at why, and the same disagreement returns on the next pull request.

Accept that a decision may go against you. Maintainers weigh things you cannot see —
backwards compatibility, WordPress.org review, users who never file issues. A reasoned no
is an answer, not an invitation to reopen the argument elsewhere.

Do not derail a technical thread with anything unrelated to it.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or demeaning remarks. Comments about someone's identity — nationality,
gender, religion, age, disability, or anything else — have no place in an issue tracker.
Publishing private information without consent. Sustained disruption after being asked to
stop.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any space where someone is
representing this project.

## Reporting

Write to **hello@hallie.app**. Reports stay confidential, and the person who reported is
never named to the person reported.

Expect an answer. What follows depends on what happened, and ranges from a private word to
a permanent ban from the project's spaces. The maintainer decides, and is not obliged to
explain that decision publicly.
